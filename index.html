Solar Load Calculator
Web Tool
A web tool that turns a homeowner's list of appliances into a recommended system size. Use it when you confirm a buyer's package before quoting, and on site visits when a buyer adds appliances you need to account for.
Why it was built

Homeowners do not trust verbal estimates. When the system size comes from their own appliances, worked out in front of them, the number belongs to them rather than to you, and the argument about whether they need something smaller goes away.

Bookmark it on your phone now. You will want it on site visits.
On Verified Buyer, use it when a buyer sends their appliance list, to confirm the package they chose is the right size.
